The three Kept Type faces are drawn on other people's bones and say so in
their license files, which ship beside the fonts in
`Galley/Resources/web/fonts/OFL-*.txt`: Numen Title from
[Fraunces](https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces), Kept Sans from
[Figtree](https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree) (with three glyphs from
Albert Sans), and Legibility Sans from
[Atkinson Hyperlegible Next](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/),
© 2020–2024 Braille Institute of America. Atkinson Hyperlegible is a
trademark of the Braille Institute; Legibility Sans is an independent
derivative and is not endorsed by them.

The name *galley* honors the galley proof — publishing's read-only typeset
draft — and John Gruber's Markdown, whose UTI (`net.daringfireball.markdown`)
Galley declares.
